Main outcome

What the evidence shows

Nepal’s conservation record is strong and clear. Tiger numbers increased substantially, forest cover remained broadly stable, and the most important tiger habitat remained in the lowland Terai landscape.

  • Tiger recovery exceeded the international doubling target.
  • Park-level gains were strongest in Chitwan, Parsa, Banke, and Shuklaphanta.
  • Recent forest loss remained small compared with the total forest stock.

Conclusion

Why the result matters

Nepal’s progress shows that strong protection, better habitat management, and local community support can produce measurable conservation gains. The findings are not a guarantee of future success, but they are a strong and encouraging signal for policy and conservation planning.
